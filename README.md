# ✈️ Airline Passenger Satisfaction Analysis

## 🎯 Business Objective
To analyze flight data and customer feedback to determine the primary drivers of passenger satisfaction. By identifying the specific services that dictate whether a customer is "Satisfied" or "Dissatisfied," airline management can strategically allocate resources to improve the customer experience and boost loyalty.

## 📊 Key Business Insights
* **Digital Over Physical:** **Online Boarding** and **Inflight Wi-Fi** are the top predictors of happiness, outweighing physical amenities like food or legroom.
* **Economy Pain Points:** Personal travelers in Economy class represent the highest concentration of dissatisfaction.
* **Service Quality:** High ratings in Wi-Fi and boarding process almost guarantee a satisfied customer.

## 🤖 Machine Learning Model
* **Algorithm:** Random Forest Classifier
* **Performance:** 96.1% Accuracy
* **Application:** ROI-focused investment roadmap for airline infrastructure.
